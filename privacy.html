Loopling privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Loopling is a game for all ages. We do not collect, receive or share any personal information, from you or from your children.

What the game does not do

  • No adverts, no analytics or tracking, and no accounts or sign-in.
  • The game itself never sends anything over the internet.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • Stardust, the game's currency, is only earned by playing. It can't be bought.

Buying the full game

Endless Play, Chill and the first two Adventure zones are free. In the Android app you can buy the full game once, through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; we never see your name, your Google account or your payment details.

Google Play tells the app whether the full game has been bought on your Google account, so it can open the extra zones, and the app keeps a note of that on your device. Google Play's purchase system, which is built into the app, also sends Google technical information about purchases. This is why the Android app has permission to use the internet. We do not receive this information.

Parents can make Google Play ask for their approval before any purchase, with Google Family Link or the Play Store's own settings.

Your saved progress

Your stardust, stickers, Adventure stars and settings are saved only on your own device, so you can carry on where you left off. They are deleted if you uninstall the game, or if you choose Reset progress in the game's Settings.

If you have Android's own backup switched on, Android may include the game's saved progress in your device backup. That backup is made and kept by Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and we cannot see it.

Vibration

The Android app asks to use the phone's vibration for a little buzz when you make a big loop. You can switch it off in the game's Settings.

Children

Loopling is made to be suitable for children. Because we collect no personal information at all, we collect none from children. The full game's button reminds children to ask a grown-up first.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
