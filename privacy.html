Loopling privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Loopling is a game for all ages. It does not collect, send or share any personal information, from you or from your children.

What the game does not do

  • No adverts, no analytics or tracking, and no accounts or sign-in.
  • No in-app purchases. Stardust, the game's currency, is only earned by playing.
  • No internet connection. The Android app does not even ask for permission to use the internet.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Your saved progress

Your stardust, stickers, Adventure stars and settings are saved only on your own device, so you can carry on where you left off. They are deleted if you uninstall the game, or if you choose Reset progress in the game's Settings.

If you have Android's own backup switched on, Android may include the game's saved progress in your device backup. That backup is made and kept by Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and we cannot see it.

Vibration

The Android app asks to use the phone's vibration for a little buzz when you make a big loop. You can switch it off in the game's Settings.

Children

Loopling is made to be suitable for children. Because it collects no personal information at all, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
